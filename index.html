A well-known pilgrimage and heritage stop in the Anantnag area, on the way to or from Pahalgam.

A green meadow valley with pines and a stream, a popular half-day outing from Pahalgam.

Sinthan Top

A high mountain pass with wide views, loved for its scenery and cool air.
